Our Story

For three generations, our family has perfected the art of hand-pulled noodles. What began as a street-side stall in Lanzhou has evolved into a culinary experience that honors tradition while embracing innovation.

Every noodle tells a story - of heritage, of craftsmanship, of the perfect bite that balances chew and tenderness.
